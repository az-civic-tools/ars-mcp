## Section count gotchas

- **Title 16 (Elections, 359 sections)** is small but dense — campaign finance, voter registration, ballot procedures, redistricting all live here
- **Title 32 (Professions, 1,553 sections)** is huge because every licensing board has its own chapter
- **Title 48 (Special Districts, 1,635 sections)** is the largest because each district type (water, fire, library, sanitary, etc.) gets a separate chapter
- **Title 13 (Criminal Code, 980 sections)** covers all of AZ criminal law
- **Title 41 (State Government, 1,474 sections)** is everything about how the state itself is structured
